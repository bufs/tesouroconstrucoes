About Tesouro Construções

Tesouro Construções is a Portuguese construction company dedicated to building homes of lasting quality. Every project combines traditional craftsmanship with modern design, energy-efficient materials and careful attention to detail.

Portugal is one of Europe's most welcoming places to live and invest: a mild climate, safe communities, excellent food and an enviable coastline. We help international buyers feel at home from the very first visit.

Why invest with us?
  • Quality construction with modern, sustainable standards
  • English-speaking support throughout the whole process
  • Prime locations close to the sea, nature and Lisbon

Our name means "treasure constructions", and that is how we treat every home we build.

Close to everything

The villa is located in the Quinta dos Foios, the most luxurious residential development inside the Vila Nogueira de Azeitão village. Surrounded by greenery and quiet streets, it offers privacy, prestige and a true sense of community.

The charming Vila Nogueira de Azeitão, known for its wineries, cheese and regional pastries, sits at the foot of the mountains of Serra da Arrábida.

The Serra da Arrábida natural park contains Portugal's most beautiful beaches, with clear turquoise water, and breathtaking views over the ocean.

All this just 25 minutes away from Lisbon, with its culture, restaurants and life style.

A home for holidays, retirement or investment, with nature, sea and city all close by.
